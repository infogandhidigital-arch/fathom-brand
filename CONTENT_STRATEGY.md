# FATHOM — Content Strategy

## Content Pillars

### 1. Original Research
- Data collection and analysis
- Surveys, interviews, experiments
- Public datasets analyzed freshly
- Frameworks and models
- **Format:** Long-form articles, whitepapers, reports
- **Frequency:** 2-4 per month

### 2. AI & Technology Deep Dives
- How specific AI models actually work
- Limitations and capabilities (honest)
- Real business applications
- Technical explanations for non-technical people
- **Format:** Article, explainer, case study
- **Frequency:** 2-3 per week

### 3. Marketing & Growth Analysis
- What actually works vs. what doesn't
- Analysis of failed strategies
- Trend autopsy (why things faded)
- Performance data and case studies
- **Format:** Article, case study, data visualization
- **Frequency:** 2-3 per week

### 4. Business & Strategy
- How to apply insights to decisions
- Frameworks for thinking
- Strategic perspectives
- Decision-making guides
- **Format:** Article, guide, framework
- **Frequency:** 1-2 per week

### 5. Curated Signals
- What's actually happening in AI/marketing
- News with original perspective
- Weekly/monthly digests
- **Format:** Newsletter, social posts, digest
- **Frequency:** Daily (social), weekly (digest)

## Publishing Cadence

### Daily
- 1-2 posts on social (research findings, insights)
- Engagement with community

### Weekly
- 3-5 long-form articles
- 1 newsletter (original writing or curated)
- 1 data-driven post

### Monthly
- 1 major research report
- 1 analysis piece
- 1 framework/playbook

## Content Standards

### Research Posts
- ✓ Primary data or original analysis
- ✓ Methodology explained
- ✓ Data visualization
- ✓ Conclusion with implications
- ✓ No affiliate links
- ✓ Clear, direct writing

### Analysis Posts
- ✓ Evidence-based perspective
- ✓ Acknowledgment of counterarguments
- ✓ Clear reasoning
- ✓ Actionable takeaways
- ✓ Sources cited

### Explainers
- ✓ Complex idea simplified
- ✓ Visual aids included
- ✓ Analogies that work
- ✓ Practical application
- ✓ Link to deeper resources

### Curation
- ✓ Original commentary (not summary)
- ✓ Why it matters
- ✓ Connection to broader trends
- ✓ Your take, not just hot take

## Topics to Own

### AI
- How LLMs actually work
- AI limitations (as important as capabilities)
- Real ROI from AI adoption
- AI in marketing (specific, not generic)
- The economics of AI
- Safety, alignment, and responsibility

### Marketing
- What marketing trends actually mean
- Performance data analysis
- Channel deep dives
- Content strategy that works
- Brand building in 2025+
- Metrics that matter

### Growth
- Sustainable growth vs. hype
- Unit economics
- CAC and LTV analysis
- Market timing and positioning
- B2B vs. B2C dynamics

### Strategy
- Decision frameworks
- Business model analysis
- Competitive positioning
- Long-term thinking
- Risk assessment

## Audience Segmentation

### Primary
- **Founders** (early-stage, Series A/B)
- **Marketing leaders** (VP/CMO level)
- **Product managers** (especially in AI/growth)
- **Business strategists**

### Secondary
- **Investors** (understanding markets, trends)
- **Consultants** (using research in client work)
- **Educators** (teaching AI/business)

## Distribution

### Owned
- Newsletter (email)
- Website/blog
- RSS feed

### Social
- Threads/X (research findings, live thinking)
- LinkedIn (professional perspective)
- Occasional other platforms

### Earned
- Media mentions
- Citations in other publications
- Speaking opportunities
- Research partnerships

## Success Metrics

### Engagement
- Newsletter open rate: 35%+ (industry: 25%)
- Newsletter click rate: 10%+ (industry: 5%)
- Social engagement rate: 3%+
- Avg. time on article: 4+ minutes

### Growth
- Subscriber growth: 10% month-over-month
- Social followers: 5% month-over-month
- Media mentions: 5+ per month
- Inbound partnerships/speaking: 2+ per month

### Authority
- Research cited elsewhere
- Backlinks from quality publications
- Industry recognition
- Speaking invitations
- Consulting/partnership inquiries

## Anti-Patterns

❌ Don't write about something just because it's trending
❌ Don't chase engagement over substance
❌ Don't publish without original thinking
❌ Don't affiliate link or shill products
❌ Don't claim certainty you don't have
❌ Don't ignore contradictory evidence
❌ Don't write the same take as everyone else
❌ Don't optimize for SEO over quality
