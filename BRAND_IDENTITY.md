# FATHOM — Brand Identity

## Logo Direction

### Concept: Depth Symbol
The letter **F** combined with a depth indicator — representing understanding what lies beneath the surface.

### Visual Variations
1. **Primary Mark** — Minimal, geometric F with subtle depth
2. **Icon Form** — Standalone symbol for favicon, social, app
3. **Wordmark** — Clean sans-serif typography

## Color Palette

### Primary
- **Deep Navy** `#1a2940` — Trust, depth, intelligence
- **Active Blue** `#0066cc` — Energy, clarity, actionable
- **Off-White** `#f8f9fa` — Clean, minimal, modern

### Secondary
- **Accent Teal** `#00a8a8` — Insight, forward-thinking
- **Neutral Gray** `#6b7280` — Context, secondary information
- **Alert Red** `#dc2626` — Critical information, caution

## Typography

### Headlines
- **Font:** Inter or Relative (geometric, clean, modern)
- **Weight:** 700 (bold)
- **Size:** 32px - 56px
- **Treatment:** All caps for major headings, title case for sections

### Body Copy
- **Font:** Inter or Relative
- **Weight:** 400 (regular)
- **Size:** 16px (desktop), 14px (mobile)
- **Line Height:** 1.6

### Data/Code
- **Font:** Monospace (JetBrains Mono, Fira Code)
- **Weight:** 500
- **Size:** 12px - 14px

## Visual Style

### Aesthetic Principles
- Minimalist and intentional
- Data-driven visualization
- Whitespace as a design element
- Geometric, clean shapes
- No unnecessary ornamentation

### Photography/Imagery
- Avoid stock photos
- Use data visualizations
- Highlight real research, real people
- Black and white or monochromatic when possible
- Subtle color accents only

## Tone & Voice

### Written Communication
- **Direct:** Get to the point
- **Precise:** Use exact language
- **Evidence-based:** Show the work
- **Accessible:** Complex ideas, clear words
- **Confident:** No unnecessary hedging
- **Humble:** Open to being wrong

### Key Phrases
- "Understand what matters"
- "Cut through the noise"
- "Think deeper"
- "Evidence-driven"
- "Original insight"
- "No shortcuts"

## Brand Applications

### Website
- Clean navigation
- Typography-first design
- Data visualizations embedded
- Fast, minimal code
- Accessibility first

### Social Media
- Threads/X (Twitter): Research findings, direct insights
- LinkedIn: Professional perspective, business applications
- Newsletter: Original writing, longer form

### Content
- Long-form research articles
- Data visualizations
- Frameworks and playbooks
- Interviews and case studies
- Original analysis

### Collateral
- Email signatures: Clean, minimal
- Business cards: Matte, typography-heavy
- Reports: Professional, data-forward
- Presentations: Minimal slides, maximum substance

## Restrictions

### What Fathom Does NOT Do
- No "AI is coming for your job" fearmongering
- No clickbait headlines
- No generic listicles
- No affiliate marketing spam
- No unsubstantiated claims
- No light takes on serious topics

## Brand Evolution

Fathom should feel:
- **Year 1:** Respected research publication
- **Year 2:** Trusted community and voice
- **Year 3:** Foundation for products/services
- **Year 5:** Authority in AI + marketing + business
